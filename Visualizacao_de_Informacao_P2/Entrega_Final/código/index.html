AN EDITOR’S NOTE

Reports, not proof.

This archive contains self-reported observations, not verified evidence of extraterrestrial activity. Country and shape can be missing; unknown values remain visible rather than being silently discarded.

Dates use the month/day/year format supplied in the CSV. Invalid years are excluded. Reports without valid coordinates still contribute to the streamgraph and witness section.
